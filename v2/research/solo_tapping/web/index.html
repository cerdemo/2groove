THE WORKING HYPOTHESIS

Local structure, shared across channel counts.

01 / LEARN

Paired local motifs

Full grooves and all their real human interpretations are positives. Other sources supply weak negatives. Dictionaries and scalers see training data only; style is not a learning target.

02 / MEASURE

More than copied onsets

1-, 2- and 4-beat windows retain onset, velocity, microtiming and metre-relative order. Retention, unsupported additions, phase, order and density are scored separately.

03 / EVOLVE

Different ways to keep a groove

MAP-Elites mutates existing elites across density × syncopation cells. Insertion, deletion, shifts and expressive edits compete under tempo-dependent cyclic hand constraints.
